## 新列表说明

Short description:

Typeset Markdown for WeChat with themes, rich-text copying, image exports, and optional note layout enhancements.

Long description:

Version 3.19.0 is an independently maintained, rebuilt Markdown typesetting workbench for desktop Obsidian. Prepare WeChat Official Account articles with 14 curated layouts in seven scenarios, five recoverable historical themes, font/background controls and phone-width preview. Copy rich text or export HTML, complete long images and segmented images. Optional note-layout enhancements are separate and disabled by default. Preview formatting does not rewrite Markdown; explicit image-caption edits update the source. Free and AGPL-3.0-or-later; requires Obsidian 1.7.2+. No account, payment, telemetry, AI service or automatic publishing. Article images may be fetched from their original hosts during rendering, copying and export. Personal Contact embeds the maintainer's QR image offline. Historical provenance and dependency licenses remain documented in README, NOTICE and THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.

Categories: Formatting (primary), Export, Images. Payments: Free. No new listing screenshots, ownership migration or other project entries are involved.
